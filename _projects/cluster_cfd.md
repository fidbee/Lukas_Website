The rocketry team recently got access to a computing cluster that is orders of magnitude more powerful than the personal PCs that we were previously simulating on. This opens the door for much more advanced and iterative CFD simulations. Due to my previous hardware constraints, this is my first exposure to CFD. I'm excited to get introduced to CFD and learn how to effectively set up and run large batches of ANSYS simulations on a computing cluster. I will update this page as progress gets made.
